What you'll explore

Four Taping Systems. One Smarter Clinical Approach.

Explore key taping systems and the different ways they may be used to support movement, stability and rehabilitation goals.

01 / ELASTIC

Kinesiology Taping: Support Movement

Explore applications commonly used for muscle support, movement awareness and functional rehabilitation.

02 / SUPPORT

Rigid Taping: Add Stability

Understand how firmer tape may be used when external support and movement restriction are part of the plan.

03 / ADAPTIVE

Semi-Elastic Taping: Balance Support & Motion

Look at approaches that balance support with selected movement needs in rehabilitation settings.

04 / PERFORMANCE

Sports Taping: Prepare for Performance

Explore taping considerations relevant to athletes, training environments and return-to-activity goals.

Topics at a glance

From Tape Basics to Clinical Application.

Explore a broad set of foundational and applied themes. The live session's final demonstration sequence may vary.

01. The Foundations: Skin, Fascia & Purpose

Purpose of taping, basic application principles, skin preparation, positioning and precautions to consider before application.

02. Understanding tape types

How kinesiology, rigid, semi-elastic and sports tapes differ in their properties and common use contexts.

03. Taping effects and clinical reasoning

Think through the intended outcome, movement needs and clinical reasoning that guide technique selection.

04. Muscles, tendons and ligaments

Demonstration themes involving muscle support, tendon-related presentations and external support considerations.

05. Common musculoskeletal applications

Examples may include shoulder, knee, ankle and spine-related applications, based on the demonstration plan.

06. Sports and functional rehabilitation

Considerations for athletic activity, training, functional movement and return-to-activity goals.

07. Posture and biomechanics

Explore how taping may be considered alongside movement assessment and broader rehabilitation planning.

08. Combined and advanced approaches

Observe how techniques may be adapted or combined for different goals, where appropriate to the case.

09. Case-based application

Connect technique choice with the presentation, treatment objective and the person's wider rehabilitation plan.
